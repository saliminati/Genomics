# Virtual NGS Lab — Project Instructions

## Role
Help develop an interactive educational web lab for an undergraduate student with basic biology knowledge.

## Product
Virtual NGS Lab — Module 1: Bacterial Culture to Purified DNA.

## Core learning model
Every screen should follow:
1. GET READY — what should I have ready?
2. PERFORM — what do I do?
3. WHY — what is happening biologically/chemically?
4. CHECK — did I do it correctly?
5. RECORD — update the electronic lab notebook.

## Required sample tracking
Use sample ID `E_COLI_001` throughout:
Colony → Culture → Cells → DNA → Library → iSeq → FASTQ → Analysis.

## Module 1 locked workflow
1. Welcome & orientation
2. Prepare for colony selection
3. Select a well-isolated colony
4. Prepare 2 mL nutrient broth and transfer colony
5. Incubate 37°C for 24 h
6. Harvest bacterial cells
7. Prepare for Quick-DNA Miniprep Plus extraction
8. Resuspend pellet: simulation assumption 1–5×10^6 cells, 200 µL
9. Add 200 µL BioFluid & Cell Buffer (Red) + 20 µL Proteinase K; mix; 55°C for 10 min
10. Add 1 volume Genomic Binding Buffer; with 420 µL digested sample, add 420 µL
11. Bind to Zymo-Spin IIC-XLR column; ≥12,000×g, 1 min; discard flow-through
12. DNA Pre-Wash: 400 µL; 1 min spin; empty collection tube
13. g-DNA Wash: 700 µL; 1 min spin; empty collection tube
14. Final g-DNA Wash: 200 µL; 1 min spin; discard collection tube/flow-through
15. Elute: ≥50 µL DNA Elution Buffer; 5 min; 1 min spin
16. QC: simulated Qubit 18.4 ng/µL, 50 µL elution = 920 ng total; separate simulated A260/A230 = 2.1
17. Complete → Module 2: Nextera library preparation

## Interaction requirements
- Students make decisions rather than watch a video.
- Include virtual bench preparation with required items and distractors.
- Include realistic tube/sample labeling.
- Include appropriate pipette/device choices.
- Include simulated mistakes and corrective feedback.
- Keep calculations only where educationally useful.
- Maintain an electronic lab notebook.
- Clearly distinguish retained material from discarded material.
- Show the biological state of DNA at every stage.

## Safety/accuracy
- Do not invent manufacturer conditions.
- Do not silently substitute another extraction kit.
- For E. coli, use the Biological Fluids & Cells workflow of Quick-DNA Miniprep Plus.
- Do not describe Qubit as a purity instrument; Qubit gives concentration.
- Treat the purity value as a separate simulated QC measurement.
- Do not invent upstream centrifuge conditions if they are not sourced from an approved SOP.
- The web app is an educational simulation, not a replacement for an approved laboratory SOP.

## Design direction
Clean modern scientific UI, undergraduate-friendly, visual but not childish:
- progress indicator
- left module navigation
- central interactive work area
- right electronic lab notebook
- persistent sample ID
- feedback after decisions
- final module summary
